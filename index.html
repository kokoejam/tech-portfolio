About

Technology should produce measurable outcomes, not technical complexity.

I specialise in building machine learning systems and cloud architectures that improve security, reduce operational cost, and automate decisions that used to depend on manual review. My work combines AI, AWS, analytics, and automation into solutions that run in production, not just in a demo.

I care most about the gap between a model that performs well on paper and a system that holds up against real transaction volume, real attackers, and real infrastructure cost pressure. Closing that gap - through feature engineering, monitoring, and sound cloud architecture - is where most of my time goes.

Background: before moving into AI/ML and cloud engineering, I worked across oil and gas management and data analytics, which is part of why I default to measuring engineering work in business terms - cost saved, risk reduced, uptime protected - rather than in isolation.

01 / Fraud detection & risk-based authentication

Fraud Detection Platform

Business problem

Banks lose significant revenue to payment fraud, and traditional rule-based systems generate excessive false positives - blocking legitimate customers as often as they catch fraud.

Solution

An ensemble fraud detection platform combining supervised and anomaly detection models with adaptive, risk-based authentication, so the response to a transaction scales with how risky it actually is.

Results
  • 98.7% detection accuracy
  • 0.96 F1 score
  • 70% fewer false positives than the rule-based baseline
  • 99% reduction in unauthorised access
